# Personal website

Kai Engelhardt's professional profile, writing, and independent apps.

## Language

**App**: One of Kai's own App Store products, presented in the app directory
and on its own detail page.

**Career point**: One company engagement, educational experience, or selected
independent milestone in Kai's professional timeline. A company engagement may
contain several successive roles; a career point may have photos and captions.

**Article**: A piece of writing published in the blog.

**Recents**: The blog view showing its ten latest articles.

**Archive**: The complete blog listing, grouped by publication year.

**About**: Kai's personal introduction and interests beyond professional history.

**Resume**: Kai's professional history, presented as a career timeline and an
independently maintained downloadable PDF.
